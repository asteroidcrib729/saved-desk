Connect your browser account

Open SavedDesk and click Connect for Instagram or X first.

Approve the account you already use in this browser profile. Only the selected platform’s session goes directly to SavedDesk on this PC.

Pairing is saved only in this extension on this browser profile. Cookies are never saved here. Keep SavedDesk open while connecting.

Checking the desktop request…

SavedDesk verifies the account before marking it connected. Passwords, browser history, and other sites are never requested.
