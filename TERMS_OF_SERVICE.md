## 2. What the application provides

SavedDesk is a Windows application for downloading supported content, maintaining a local library and viewing or playing saved media. Availability depends on the platform, the content, your access rights, installed tools, your browser and Windows capabilities.

The application currently supports Instagram, X, YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest and supplied Discord attachment links through the documented routes. Not every URL, media type, account or private resource is supported. Spotify and Reddit downloading are not supported.

Instagram and X support documented verified-account routes. Facebook, TikTok and Pinterest are limited to publicly available share links; private/restricted content is outside scope. Discord uses a supplied media attachment without SavedDesk account authentication. The [README user guide](README.md) contains complete installation, account, download and Settings instructions.

Optional Google sign-in currently verifies identity only. It does not provide permission to download private videos or transfer Google OAuth credentials to download workers. Some routes use a separately approved browser session. A successful sign-in or browser approval does not guarantee extraction or grant rights over someone else's content.

SavedDesk is not affiliated with or endorsed by the supported platforms, Google, Microsoft or their respective owners. Their names and marks identify compatible services.
